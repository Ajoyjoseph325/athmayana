Welcome to one of India's most sacred Himalayan pilgrimages. Journey through the spiritually rich Kumaon Himalayas, where ancient temples, mystical caves, sacred lakes, revered ashrams, and majestic Himalayan peaks come together in an experience of devotion, discovery, and inner peace.

Your pilgrimage begins amidst the serene foothills of Kumaon with the blessings of Neem Karoli Baba's Kainchi Dham, the tranquil beauty of Bhimtal, the ancient Katarmal Sun Temple, Kasar Devi, Chitai Golu Devta Temple, and the timeless Jageshwar Dham, before ascending into the pristine Adi Kailash Region.

Nestled close to the Indo–Tibetan frontier, this sacred Himalayan landscape is home to Adi Kailash, the serene Parvati Sarovar, and the awe-inspiring Om Parvat, where nature itself forms the sacred symbol ॐ upon the mountainside. Revered for centuries, this region offers pilgrims a rare opportunity to experience the divine amidst the untouched grandeur of the Himalayas.

As your journey descends through the mystical Patal Bhuvaneshwar Cave Temple and the revered Mahavatar Babaji Cave, it concludes in the charming hill town of Nainital, where the sacred Naina Devi Shakti Peeth, the tranquil waters of Naini Lake, and the vibrant Tibetan Market provide a peaceful and memorable finale to this extraordinary pilgrimage.

This is more than a journey across the Himalayas—it is an invitation to slow down, embrace the silence of the mountains, and return home carrying their blessings within your heart.
