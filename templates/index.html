Any Questions?

What is included in the Kailash Mansarovar package?

Our Kailash Mansarovar package includes accommodation, daily meals, transportation, permits, visa assistance, experienced tour guides, medical support, and pilgrimage assistance throughout the journey. Package inclusions may vary depending on the route and package selected.

How many days does the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra take?

The duration of the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra usually ranges from 10 to 18 days depending on the route selected, such as Nepal route, helicopter route, or overland route. We offer flexible packages to suit different schedules and preferences.

Do I need a visa or special permits for the yatra?

Yes, Kailash Mansarovar Yatra requires special travel permits and visa approvals. Our team handles the complete documentation process and assists you with all required paperwork for a hassle-free pilgrimage.

Is the Kailash Mansarovar Yatra physically challenging?

The yatra involves high-altitude travel and moderate trekking, especially during the sacred parikrama. Basic physical fitness is recommended, and our team provides proper guidance, acclimatization support, and medical assistance throughout the journey.

What is the best time to visit Kailash Mansarovar?

The best season for Kailash Mansarovar Yatra is from May to September when weather conditions are relatively stable, roads are accessible, and the mountain views are clear, making the pilgrimage more comfortable and memorable.

What documents are required for booking the package?

You typically need a valid passport, passport-size photographs, identification documents, and completed application forms. Our team will guide you step-by-step regarding all required documents during the booking process.

Are helicopter packages available for Kailash Mansarovar?

Yes, we offer helicopter packages for pilgrims who prefer a faster and more comfortable journey. Helicopter routes reduce travel time while still allowing you to complete the sacred darshan and parikrama experience.

How can I book my Kailash Mansarovar package?

You can book your Kailash Mansarovar package by contacting our travel consultants through phone, WhatsApp, or online enquiry forms. Our team will help you choose the best package, complete documentation, and confirm your journey smoothly.
