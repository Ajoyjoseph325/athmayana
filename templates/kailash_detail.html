Day 08 - Lake Manasarovar – Darchen

Drive from Lake Manasarovar to Darchen, the base camp for the Kailash Parikrama. Rest and prepare for the sacred circumambulation of Mount Kailash.

Day 09 - Darchen – Yamadwar – Derapuk

Begin the Kailash Parikrama. Trek from Darchen through Yamadwar (Gate of God of Death) to Derapuk, with spectacular views of the north face of Mount Kailash.

Day 10 - Derapuk – Drolma La Pass – Zutul Puk

The most challenging day — crossing the Drolma La Pass at 5,630 m, the highest point of the Parikrama. Descend to Zutul Puk for overnight stay.

Day 11 - Zutul Puk – Darchen – Saga

Complete the Kailash Parikrama and drive back to Saga for overnight rest. A day of spiritual fulfilment and physical recovery.

Day 12 - Saga – Kerung (Drive)

Return drive from Saga to Kerung. Begin the journey back towards Nepal through the scenic Tibetan plateau.

Day 13 - Kerung – Kathmandu (Drive)

Cross back into Nepal at Rasuwagadhi and drive to Kathmandu. Evening free for shopping or personal time.

Day 14 - Departure from Kathmandu

Transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport for departure. Carry back the blessings and divine memories of Kailash Mansarovar Yatra.
